FAQ

What is a Kalshi whale?

A trader placing a single large order — here, $10,000 or more — on a Kalshi prediction market. Kalshi trades are anonymous, so we track the orders, not the people.

How is this tracked?

Kalshi publishes every fill. We group fills with the same market, side and timestamp into one taker order and list those of $10k+. Near-certain sweeps at 97¢+ and multi-leg parlays are excluded as noise.

Do whales actually win?

Each trade is marked won or lost when its market settles. The win rate is compared with the price paid (the market-implied probability): whales only "beat the market" if they win more often than the price implied.
